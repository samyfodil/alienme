# Credits

AlienMe is made by [Samy Fodil](https://github.com/samyfodil). The code is under the licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE); the 3D models are by the artists below and keep their own licences.

Changes made to the models: the props were re-encoded with gltf-transform (WebP textures) and some had their normal and ORM maps removed. The game restyles every model as it draws it: toon shading, outlines, a pastel tint, bevelled edges, and new colours for clothes, hair and skin.
